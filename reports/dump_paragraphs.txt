24: ABSTRACT [HAS 2 DRAWING]
25: This project presents an AI-based sales forecasting system for a general retail-business scenario. The objective is to predict future sales using historical sales records, promotional information and seasonal patterns. Sales forecasting is a regression problem because the target variable, sales, is a continuous numerical value. [HAS 2 DRAWING]
26: The system uses a Kaggle-style Sales Forecast Dataset containing Date, Store, Sales and Promo columns. Past sales values, promotional indicators, rolling statistics and seasonality features are used as input features. Two machine-learning techniques are implemented: Linear Regression and XGBoost Regressor. The models are evaluated primarily using Root Mean Squared Error (RMSE), along with MAE and R-squared score. [HAS 2 DRAWING]
27: The model with the lowest RMSE is automatically selected and used to forecast sales for the next six months. The system generates CSV reports and visual charts for historical sales, model comparison and future forecasting. The outcome helps a business plan inventory, staffing, promotions and demand management more effectively. [HAS 2 DRAWING]
